## Title

Sets, Logic, Relations and Functions | SRAI Book 1, Lesson 2

## Description

Lesson 2 of SRAI Book 1 develops the formal language of sets, logic, relations and functions, then connects it to databases, knowledge systems, reproducible computation and transparent decision rules.

You will learn how to declare universes and domains, distinguish membership from subsethood, evaluate logical implication without confusing it with causation, and test whether a relation is a partial or total function.
