# Lesson 7 — Singular Value Decomposition, PCA, and Dimensionality Reduction

`PU-B01-C07` develops the mathematics and decision controls required to reduce dimensionality without hiding consequential information.

## Learning outcomes

- decompose a real matrix as `A = UΣVᵀ`;
- verify reconstruction and orthogonality;
- construct and evaluate rank-k approximations;
- derive PCA from the SVD of centered data;
- interpret explained variance without turning it into a causal claim; and
- document what a compressed representation preserves and loses.

## Included materials

- corrected lesson in DOCX and PDF;
- exercises with explained solutions in DOCX and PDF;
- executive brief in DOCX and PDF;
- executed companion notebook with saved outputs;
- editable 12-slide teaching presentation; and
- video narration and presenter run of show.

All examples use synthetic instructional data. The lesson emphasizes reproducible preprocessing, reconstruction evidence, subgroup and rare-event checks, and accountable stopping rules.
