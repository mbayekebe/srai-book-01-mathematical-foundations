How much information can we remove without weakening the decision?

Lesson 7 of SRAI Book 1 introduces Singular Value Decomposition, Principal Component Analysis and dimensionality reduction through a decision-first lens.

Learners move from the identity `A = UΣVᵀ` to rank-k approximation, PCA on centered data, explained variance and a national-indicator case. The central discipline is simple: report not only what the reduced representation retains, but also what it removes, which observations carry the largest residuals and when compression should be revised or stopped.

The lesson package includes the chapter, explained exercises, executive brief, executed notebook, teaching deck and video-production materials.
